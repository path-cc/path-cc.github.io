The National Science Foundation AI Infrastructure Hubs Solicitation (NSF 26-513) offers Hubs to “leverage cyberinfrastructure investments” and integrate with national NSF funded resources through PATh. Please do not hesitate (or wait too long) to contact us at ai-hub-proposals@path-cc.io with questions or requests for letters of support regarding your AI Hub proposed project.

The PATh team has experience offering guidance to projects during the proposal phase and for offering software tools and services during the operation phase.
